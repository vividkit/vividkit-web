# Facts

- The landing page sells VividKit Pipeline Packs as practical implementation kits for concrete workflows, not as premium articles or generic courses.
- The first featured pack is the Workflow Optimization Pipeline: cmux + NotebookLM + Obsidian + Codex/Claude Code.
- The primary audience is automation builders and AI power users who already use Claude, Codex, NotebookLM, or Obsidian but do not yet have a repeatable operating workflow.
- The page promise is that buyers get a working pipeline setup with playbook, step-by-step guide, templates, artifact examples, failure modes, and an operating rhythm.
- The first CTA is pre-sell or waitlist oriented, used to validate demand before building real checkout, account, entitlement, or paywall infrastructure.
- The early access price anchor is $9 per pack, framed as a limited first-cohort price for a self-serve implementation kit.
- The $9 early access pack includes pack files, pack updates, and private buyer Discord access for shared Q&A, updates, and artifact issue reports, but it does not include 1:1 setup or debugging support.
- The page may mention paid setup review or workflow audit as a future upsell, but the first implementation does not need to build that purchase flow.
- The first implementation includes one landing page, navigation or CTA entry points, bilingual EN/VI copy, and static pack/pricing sections.
- VividKit Pipeline Packs should be implemented as a separate project, not inside the existing vividkit-web repository.
- The separate project should use Astro for the landing site and Cloudflare Worker for future integration points.
- Real checkout, gated content, subscription membership, account login, entitlement checks, and Discord automation are out of scope for the first implementation.
- The visual direction is a practical product page: dense, concrete, pipeline-focused, with simple system diagrams and artifact previews, avoiding generic SaaS hero copy.
- The page keeps the VividKit brand and may use VividKit Pipeline Packs or VividKit Automation Lab as the paid product umbrella.
